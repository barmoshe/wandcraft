## Version page

**Promotional text (170)**
Program a wand from spells, boosts and triggers, then fight your way through a glitching grove. Every run hands you new parts; the build is yours to write.

**Description**
Wandcraft is a pixel art roguelite where your wand is a tiny program.

Spells fire left to right. Boosts power up every spell to their right. Triggers join two spells, so one bolt ends and the next begins where it lands. Every reward is a part, and the wand editor is where a run is won: rearrange, combine and rewrite it between fights.

Enemies are built to make you edit. Shields block everything but pierce. Armour soaks damage until a blast tears it off. Wards swallow whole hits until a shock strips them. Read the room, change the wand, and try again.

- Twin-stick controls made for one phone in two hands, with auto-fire when you want it
- A first run that teaches the wand editor one lesson at a time
- A branching map where every door shows what is behind it
- Relics that bend the rules, forges that compile spells into something new, and a Glitch Door with a price
- Two bosses: one copies your own wand and casts it back at you
- Source Fragments from every run unlock more spells, relics, runes and wands
- Bug Reports: harder runs that stack, for when you have won
- No ads, no purchases, no account, no tracking. It plays offline.

Made by one developer. All art is drawn in code and all music and sound are generated in code.
