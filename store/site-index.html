Wandcraft

Code your wand, beat the bug.

A pixel art roguelite where your wand is a tiny program. Spells fire left to right, boosts power up the spells to their right, and triggers join two spells into one. Every reward is a part; the wand editor is where a run is won.

Free on iPhone. No ads, no purchases, no account, no tracking. It plays offline.
