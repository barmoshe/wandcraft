Wandcraft: Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

Wandcraft is a game made by Bar Moshe. This page explains what the game does with your information. The short version: nothing leaves your device.

What the game collects

Nothing. Wandcraft has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet while you play.

What the game stores on your device

To let you continue a run and keep your progress, the game saves a few small files inside its own storage on your device:

  • the run in progress (your wands, spells, relics and the room you are in)
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, auto-fire, screen shake)
  • lifetime numbers and unlocks (runs played, wins, Source Fragments, the Codex)

These files never leave your device and are not shared with anyone, including me. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before a version of the game that does anything different is released.
