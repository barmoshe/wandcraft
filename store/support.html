Wandcraft: Support

Something wrong, or an idea? Email 1barmoshe1@gmail.com with your device, your iOS version and what happened. The version number is at the bottom left of the title screen.

How do I play?

  • Move with your left thumb. Aim with your right, or let auto-fire pick the nearest enemy.
  • Tap DASH to slip through bullets.
  • Tap the bag button to open the wand editor. Spells fire left to right; boosts power up the spells to their right; a trigger joins the spell on its left to the one on its right.

My sound is off

Check the Sound and Music switches in the pause menu and your phone's volume. On iPhone, the Ring/Silent switch (or the Action button's silent mode) mutes games too: set it to Ring.

Can I start over?

Pause, then Abandon run. Deleting the app also deletes your progress and unlocks.
