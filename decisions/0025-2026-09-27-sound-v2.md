# 0025. Sound v2: every sound, the score and the audio runtime redesigned

- Date: 2026-09-27
- Status: Accepted
- Builds on ADR 0019 (D8), whose rule it keeps: every sound is generated in code, original and deterministic. It replaces all of D8's content and most of its runtime. Research and full specs: `research/sound-v2.md` (the sonic brief, synth toolkit, cue-spec, music spec, mix, DOG) and `research/sound-v2-runtime-audit.md` (event coverage and the runtime design).

## Context
Bar's verdict on design v3 was that the game "looks/sounds unfinished". D8's effects were thin single-oscillator chip blips, and measurement found worse than taste:
- The booms, slams and `hurt` lived below what a phone plays: booms and slams lost 6 to 8.5 dB through a phone-speaker filter.
- The most frequent sound, `hit`, sat at 12 to 20 kHz.
- `crit` shared its band with ice, and the telegraphs shared theirs with the music lead.
- The 112 bpm Foundry broke the whole-sample bar rule.
- There was no shared theme.

The runtime audit found that:
- Oldest-first voice stealing cut `kill_big`, `boom` and even `hurt`.
- The ambience ignored the settings.
- The bar sync ran on wall-clock time.
- Boss wind-ups ended up to 0.68 s early.
- About 30 gameplay events were silent, and 10 shipped files were never played.

## Decision
- **A sonic identity:** "code you can hear". Materials (gem-glass, grimoire stone, gold filigree, furnace iron, spore) are struck by a pitched digital "sigil" pulse. Threats are louder than the player's own output. Weight comes from saturated 200-900 Hz harmonics that phones play. There is one key per room. Glitch is kept for corruption.
- **A leitmotif:** "The Incantation" (1-5-b3-4-5 | b7-1) runs through the title, every area, the bosses and every stinger.
- **A shared synth library, `tools/lib_dsp.gd`:**
  - multi-segment envelopes and pitch envelopes;
  - PolyBLEP oscillators and the sigil;
  - a Simper SVF with control-rate modulation, RBJ EQ and a Butterworth high-pass;
  - saturation, "phantom" low-end harmonics, bit-crush and stutter;
  - FM with material ratios, Karplus-Strong and a modal resonator bank (stone, wood, brass, glass, plate, bell);
  - grains, whooshes, delays and combs, and a Freeverb-lite room.
- **Effects (`tools/gen_audio.gd`):** 152 cue ids in 325 files, with 8 loudness families baked into the files (momentary loudness, with true peak at or under -1 dBTP).
  - The busiest cues have variants, and the dense casts get `_heavy` versions.
  - The telegraphs are three attack classes (shot, charge, slam) plus boss signatures. Each is a 1.5 s master played from an offset, so it ends on the release frame.
  - The Deadlock beam and the Loop's trail are seamless loops.
  - `Loudness` gains `momentary_max`, `true_peak_db` and `band_share`; `tools/audio_report.gd` audits the DOG.
- **Music (`tools/gen_music.gd`):**
  - A new score in 32 kHz mono, with the reverb and chorus baked into the files. The Foundry is now F minor at 125 bpm.
  - The boss and mini-boss intros are folded into their loop files, so the bar clock can read the playback position. The boss also gains a `p3` layer.
  - The ambience beds are 22 kHz stereo.
  - New stingers: `boss_down`, `world`, and per-area `clear` and `reward` variants.
  - Every loop file ends with one guard sample. `tools/audio.sh` writes the loop window gen_music reports into each `.import` file.
- **Runtime (`game/scripts/autoload/audio.gd`):**
  - One `CUES` table drives class, priority, cap, gap, gain, jitter, pan and walk for every cue.
  - A queued voice manager starts at most 6 new voices a frame on the web (8 native). Repeats within a frame are merged, the weakest voice is stolen first, and one critical voice is reserved for `hurt`/`die`.
  - Our own variant picker replaces `AudioStreamRandomizer`.
  - World sounds pan by screen position.
  - Snapshots for menu, death and low HP, a hit-stop duck, and a low-HP heartbeat with its own setting.
  - Kill, coin and gulp streaks walk the area key's minor pentatonic.
  - A bar clock read from the music's own position, bar-quantised layers, and intensity with a 2 s hold.
  - The buses follow the spec: Critical goes to Master, a Critical-keyed compressor sits on SFX, and stingers have their own bus. With the music's space baked in, 3 bus effects are always on, down from 5.
- **Hooks:** every priority-1 and priority-2 event in the audit is wired across the world, spell runner, enemies, player, the four bosses and the UI screens.

## Reconciliation
The Audio Director's spec and the Game Developer's audit were reconciled: 31 runtime proposals were accepted and 2 changed.
- **Streak pitch-walks** step on the area's pentatonic, not by semitones, and hits don't walk.
- **Telegraph identity** is by attack class, not by enemy; panning says where the attack comes from.
- **The bar clock** has no output-latency term (the Developer's counter-objection, accepted).
- **The stem guard sample** is kept, with an explicit loop window per file (the Developer's objection, resolved in `tools/audio.sh`).

## Consequences
- About 50 MB of WAV in the repo (QOA in the build). A full `tools/audio.sh` takes about 3.5 minutes on Bar's Mac (effects 43 s, music 122 s), and the old sounds can be A/B'd in `shots/audio-v1/`.
- `tools/audiosheet.sh` writes `shots/audiosheet.html`, one player per shipped file, for listening reviews.
- Bar has not heard it yet. His notes become a revision pass on the generator recipes, not a swap of files.
- **Not done:** a mixer screen; music intensity from threat near the player (the enemy count only for now); runtime true-peak metering.
