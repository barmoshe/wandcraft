# 0010. v0.4 becomes the POC; the MVP is a store-ready World 1, rebuilt in place

- Date: 2026-09-24
- Status: Accepted
- Amends `scope.md` (acceptance criteria and scope for v1, see below).

## Context
Bar: "treat this as a POC and let's create MVP. The MVP will be better in every aspect. Research the design and plan."

The research is in `research/mvp-research.md`:
- an honest audit of v0.4.1
- a sourced genre teardown
- sourced production and tech research

**The audit's central finding:** the wand-programming pitch is optional. The balance bot never edits a wand and still survives 70–80%. Beyond that:
- the player's only verbs are moving and auto-fire
- runs are short
- there is no meta-progression
- `world.gd` is a god object
- relic effects are scattered through the code
- the UI is hand-placed
- art and audio are at their ceilings

Bar decided four things:
- **MVP goal:** a store-ready World 1. The rebuilt World 1 has meta-progression, runs on TestFlight and in a Google Play closed test, and has the Full Game unlock wired.
- **Art:** keep it drawn in code, but raise its ceiling.
- **Audio:** CC0 sound effects plus free-licensed music.
- **Budget:** $0.

## Decision
- **v0.4.1 is the POC.** The MVP follows `research/mvp-plan.md`, milestones M0–M11.
- **Rebuild in place inside `game/`, not in a new project.**
  - Keep: the wand engine and its tests, the tools, the export presets, the web fixes and the git history.
  - Replace: `world.gd`, the content tables, the relic checks and the UI, module by module behind the existing seams. Each old file is deleted in the milestone that replaces it, and the tests stay green on every commit.
- **Design principles** (15, each sourced in the research):
  - Program the wand; let the game handle firing (auto-fire and an aim cone).
  - A dash.
  - The wand is a linear timeline with a live preview.
  - The first wand edit comes within 2 minutes.
  - Runs last 12–18 minutes, on a branching map with the reward shown on each door.
  - Meta-progression unlocks new content with visible conditions, not stat grind.
  - A new run starts within 3 seconds of dying.
  - Enemy bullets have a reserved style, with colorblind presets.
- **Budget $0:**
  - Everything is built to store standard for free.
  - Store submission waits behind a gate that needs the accounts: Apple $99 a year and Google Play $25.
  - Music is CC0, or CC-BY credited in the game. Every audio file is recorded in a licence manifest, and a test enforces it.
- **The MVP is done** when the TestFlight and Play closed-test betas are live and the unlock's purchase and restore are verified in both sandboxes.

## Scope amendments
`scope.md` changes only through an ADR; this ADR is that change.
- **"A full run (5 worlds, 10 bosses)" is replaced for v1** by a full World 1 run of 12–18 minutes with 2 bosses. Worlds 2+ come after the MVP.
- **"The app is approved and live on both stores" moves to after World 2.** App Review won't approve an unlock for content that doesn't exist. The MVP ends at live betas.
- **New acceptance criteria:**
  - First wand edit by 120 s and first trigger by 180 s of game time (onboarding test).
  - The balance bench shows programming matters:
    - a bot that edits its wands survives 55–80%
    - a bot that never edits survives 40% or less
    - the gap between them is at least 15 points
  - 120 fps in a normal fight on the iPhone 15 Pro Max.
  - Every audio asset has a licence entry.
- **Renderer:** moving to Compatibility everywhere will be decided separately (ADR 0011), after an A/B check in M0.

## Consequences
- **Several weeks of agent work across 11 milestones.** Each one ends with a web build, an APK, a STATUS update and Bar's device check.
- **Save format:** POC saves are reset with a notice when the save format changes. Saves are versioned from then on.
- **Cut order if time runs short** (from `mvp-plan.md`): achievements go first. Never cut: onboarding into a wand edit, the dash, the editor, basic meta, the purchase wiring, or the tap and CI tests.
