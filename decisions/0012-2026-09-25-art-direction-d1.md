# 0012. Art direction rules for the MVP (D1)

- Date: 2026-09-25
- Status: Accepted
- Supersedes the outline, shading and lighting rules in ADR 0007. ADR 0007's code-drawn pipeline and Style ramps stay.

## Context
The graphics research (`research/design-research.md` §3) found the POC's art failing on figure and ground:
- **The floor was the busiest thing on screen.** Every tile had bevelled slabs and cracks.
- **The sel-out outline was nearly black on a dark floor.**
- **An automatic rim light "pillowed" every shape.**
- **Enemies breathed by fractional scaling**, which smears pixels. Elites were also scaled by 1.25.
- **Pink meant the player's magic, the enemies' bodies and the enemies' bullets all at once.**
- **Several enemies failed a grayscale value test** against the floor.

## Decision
- **The floor is quiet.** It uses a narrow band of 3 values with large, calm, low-frequency patches, dithered only where two values meet. Faint joints sit on a 2-tile grid. Small stamps (pebbles, cracks) come in clusters, with empty floor between them. Moss and tufts are desaturated.
- **Walls:** caps are lighter than the floor, faces darker.
- **Outlines:** INK on the bottom and right edges and the ground line only. Top and left edges take the neighbouring pixel's own ramp step 1. The rim light adds one step on top-facing edges only.
- **Role hues:**
  - A new `threat` ramp is reserved for enemy attacks: bullets (white core, red ring, dark rim, 9 px, normal blend, drawn above player bullets), telegraphs, muzzle flashes and spawn runes.
  - Player magic uses cool colours plus gold. Pink stays with the Glitch-bodied enemies.
- **Enemy statuses recolour along a Style ramp by brightness** in the enemy shader, instead of a `modulate` tint. Elites keep pixel-perfect size and show an outline colour instead of a 1.25 scale. Enemies bob by whole pixels.
- **`tools/artsheet.sh check`** shows every actor on a real floor patch, in grayscale and as a silhouette. New art must pass the grayscale value test.

## Consequences
- **Readability:** the room calms down and actors carry the contrast. The weaver, ram and sentry palettes were lifted to pass the value test.
- **Deferred:**
  - Front-cap y-sort occlusion: walls don't rise above their tile yet. It comes with taller walls in D5.
  - Rigged animation and the full per-actor redraw come in D6.
