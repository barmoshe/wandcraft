# 0016. Levels, room features and the 3-lane map (D5)

- Date: 2026-09-25
- Status: Accepted
- Builds on `research/design-plan.md` §4 and ADR 0015 (the encounter director).

## Context
The POC had five fight rooms, all about one screen, flat stone with pillars. The route was a door choice with no view ahead. The design wants three things:
- rooms that vary in size and ask questions of the wand
- terrain that interacts with spells (blast, burn, knockback, gravity)
- a map worth planning on

## Decision
- **18 new fight layouts** (`scripts/world/room_layouts.gd`):
  - Six S rooms (20×12), eight M rooms (30×17) and four L rooms (40×22), each tagged easy, medium or hard. Generated by a script that checks they are sound.
  - The POC's five stay in the medium pool.
  - `RoomLayouts.pool(step)` serves small, easy rooms first and medium/large, harder rooms later.
  - The camera already scrolls in rooms bigger than the view.
- **Room features, as tile ids** (so the bullet hot loop pays nothing extra):

  | Feature | What it does |
  |---|---|
  | Pit (5) | Spells fly over it; bodies can't walk it. A knockback or a Gravity pull carries fodder in, and it falls ("FELL"). Heavy enemies and bosses never fall. |
  | Bramble (6) | Blocks. Fire clears it: a burning spell or a burning blast. |
  | Cracked wall (7) | A blast opens it, revealing a secret alcove with a chest (a relic, a spell leaning rare, 30 gold). |
  | Spore pod (8) | Any hit or blast sets it off: 22 blast damage, and pods chain. It never hurts the player (no hazards that hurt you). |
  | Rune pylon (9) | A hit sends a pulse that stuns enemies nearby for 1.2 s and strips wards. 4 s cooldown. |

- **Two areas:**
  - Rooms 1–4 are the Mossy Root Cellar.
  - Rooms 5–8 are the Corrupted Grove: the same stone under a violet cast, with glitch pixels in the floor and a violet tint in the ambient light.
  - Separate music layers wait for D8.
- **The 3-lane map** (`Chapter.make_map`, saved in the run):
  - Three nodes per room step, one each for the mini-boss and the boss. From a lane you reach the lane beside it, and the doors after a room are the nodes you can reach.
  - The first two rooms are fights.
  - Nothing special (challenge, Glitch Door, altar, terminal) before the fourth room.
  - No quiet room twice in a row on a lane.
  - The middle lane before each boss is always a spring or a shop.
  - Every step has a fight.
  - A **map screen** opens by tapping the HUD strip or through `--screen=map`.
- **Special rooms:**
  - **Altar:** two epic-leaning spells and a relic; each pick costs 15% max HP.
  - **Debug Terminal:** one patch: +1 slot, 40 gold, or heal 35.

## Consequences
- **Tests:** 10 new tests (`tests/unit/test_levels_d5.gd`), 146 in all. Every layout builds in-engine with reachable sockets and doors and a walled-off secret. The feature tests cover pods chaining, brambles only burning to fire, the pylon stun and cooldown, pits, and secrets. The map rules and lane doors are tested, along with the save round trip.
- **Deferred:**
  - Doors on side walls: they stay on the top wall, since the door and camera flow assume it.
  - One-way vine gates.
  - The front-cap y-sort occlusion: walls that hide your feet. This comes with D6's taller wall art.
  - Per-area music (D8).
