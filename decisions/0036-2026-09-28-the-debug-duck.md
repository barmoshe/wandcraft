# 0036. The Debug Duck: one design for every Duck (0.24)

- Date: 2026-09-28
- Status: Accepted
- Model sheet: `research/duck-design.md`. Builds on ADR 0007 (Style ramps) and ADR 0031 (companions in the world).

## Context
Bar asked to reinvent the Duck's design, style and art. The Duck was five drawings that didn't match:
- a front-on bust for the speech box, the companion and the Workshop;
- a side-view duck for the Rubber Duck spell and its icon;
- a 5x blow-up of the face for the story panels.

It never blinked, its beak never moved in the speech box, and its face used an inline hex.

He chose the "debug duck": a glossy rubber duck with a big eye, a shine, a knowing brow, and tiny teal glasses like the hero's.

## Decision
- **`DuckArt` is the only source of Duck art.** It covers the face, the companion body, the sitting Workshop Duck, the spell's familiar and icon, and a bust in a bath for the story panels.
  - Every piece shares one head and one palette of Style ramp keys.
  - The glasses use the hero's own keys.
  - `Hud.duck_face`, `CompanionArt.duck` and `Props.familiar(&"duck")` forward to it.
- **Three moods** (plain, smug, pleased), chosen from the line's event (`DuckArt.mood_of`).
- **It blinks**, and its bill moves while it speaks, wherever it appears.
- **The face stays 14x13**, the size the residents' portraits match, so the speech box layout is unchanged.
- **Review:** the `duck` art sheet (`tools/artsheet.sh duck`), and `--panel=N` opens a story panel for shots.

## Consequences
- `test_duck_art` checks:
  - the face's size;
  - that moods, blink, talk, steps and ripples change the picture;
  - that every color is a ramp step;
  - that the spell's familiar and icon come from `DuckArt`.
- The old grids are gone, and the inline hex with them.
- **Not done:** the Duck has no new voice or new lines. This is art only.
