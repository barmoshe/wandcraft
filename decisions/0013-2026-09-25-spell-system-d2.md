# 0013. The spell system for the MVP (D2)

- Date: 2026-09-25
- Status: Accepted
- Builds on ADR 0011 (design-first re-plan) and `research/design-plan.md` §1. The catalog and wand engine from ADR 0007 stay, reshaped as below.

## Context
The POC made wand building optional by construction (`research/design-research.md` §1):
- Every reward slotted itself into the wand.
- The starting wand had 5 slots and 80 mana, so mana never ran short.
- Levels needed 3 copies, which a 15-minute run rarely finds.
- Nothing asked for a particular answer: any damage answered every enemy.

The design wants the wand to be the answer, and the program to be readable.

## Decision
- **Placing a spell is the player's call.**
  - Rewards go to the bag. The one exception is a wand that cannot cast at all, which takes a shooting spell straight away.
  - The reward screen has **TAKE & EQUIP**, which opens the editor.
- **Start small.** The start room offers two loadouts:
  - **Twig Wand:** 3 slots, 50 mana, a Mote.
  - **Stub Staff:** 2 slots, 90 mana, an Ember Bolt.
- **Growing the wand:**
  - Wand drops.
  - The forge sells **+1 slot** for 60 gold, up to 10 slots.
- **Merging:** 2 copies merge into the next level, so level 3 takes 4 copies.
- **Level 3 changes behaviour on some spells:**
  - Mote pierces.
  - Fan fires 9 bolts.
  - Needle gains crit.
  - Ember burns hotter.
  - Frost fires 3 shards.
  - Seed carries its payload at 60% mana.
  - Chorus loses its spread.
  - Firewall blocks shots for longer.
  - The editor shows an "L3:" line.
- **56 spells:**
  - **New shooting spells:** Firewall (stops enemy shots), Bitrot Spore, Hex Cursor (payloads aim at the marked enemy).
  - **New carrier:** Ping.
  - **New boosts:** Orbit, Reverse (+60%, fires behind), Siphon (mana back on a kill).
  - **New coats:** Static and Rot.
  - **New draw spell:** Pipeline.
  - **New trigger:** Sleep.
  - **Debugger runes:**
    - HEAD: a free copy of the first shooting spell.
    - IF / ELSE: branches on whether an enemy is within 60 px.
    - GOTO 1: once per cycle, jumps back to slot 1 without recharging.
    - #include: the next boost applies to every spell, at ×1.5 cost.
  - **Familiars, with caps:** Daemon (orbits and casts its payload, paying per shot), Watchdog Turret, Rubber Duck (a decoy enemies chase and shoot).
  - **Passives:** Mana Well (Cache and Regen merged), Heat Sink, Watchdog (a free cast after 1 s idle).
  - **Cut or merged:** Shatter is cut. Linger merges into Long Range.
  - **Old saves:** they map through `Catalog.ALIASES`.
- **Wand quirks:**
  - Daemon Rod: its last slot fires on its own every 3 s.
  - Debug Build: 8 slots, but runes cost double.
- **Statuses and reactions**, each with one colour and one pip, at most 3 pips per enemy:
  - Burn.
  - Chill: 3 chills freeze.
  - Static: the next hit arcs to a neighbour.
  - Bitrot: 5 stacks crash the target.
  - Thermal Shock: burn meets chill.
  - Overclocked: 2 or more statuses, +20% damage taken.
- **Resist keywords** (pierce, blast, shock) are carried on every hit, for D4's shields, armour and wards.
- **Economy:**
  - Epic odds follow a pacing offset: +1% per common, reset on an epic.
  - The shop lets you **Deprecate** one spell per visit, banning it for the run.
- **Readability:**
  - The editor shows a rotation bar: mana per rotation against regen, green when sustained, otherwise how many rotations until empty. It also shows IF/ELSE branches in the cast preview.
  - The HUD pointer follows the next slot that will be read, including on reversed wands, and the Daemon Rod's background timer.
- **The balance bot edits.**
  - `WandPlanner` (in `sim/`) takes the offer that raises a rough DPS estimate most, equips from the bag, reorders the wand and buys a forge slot.
  - The bench runs it next to a bot that never edits.

## Consequences
- **Bench (10 seeds):**
  - The editing bot survives **70%**, inside the 40–85% guardrail. Mini-boss about 61 s, boss about 41 s.
  - The bot that never edits survives **0%**.
  - Editing now decides the run. D4's enemy counters will aim for 15–30% for the non-editor, so a player who never opens the editor can still sometimes win.
- **Performance:** the bullet hot loop stays at the POC's cost (8.8–9.6 ms a tick in the 1,300-bullet stress test on the dev container). Per-node values are computed once per compiled node, and plain bolts skip the behaviour table.
- **Deferred:**
  - Corrupted rarity, "Enables" chips on cards and Compile evolutions move to D3, with the Glitch Door and duo relics.
  - Shields, armour and wards that read the keywords arrive in D4.
  - The catalog stays as code tables (per this project's CLAUDE.md).
