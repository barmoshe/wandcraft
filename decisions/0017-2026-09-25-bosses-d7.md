# 0017. The bosses: Copy-Paste copies your wand, the Loop has three phases (D7)

- Date: 2026-09-25
- Status: Accepted
- Builds on `research/design-plan.md` §5 and ADR 0015 (defences and keywords).

## Context
The POC bosses were bullet patterns. Neither one asked anything of the wand, and the bot that never edits died at the final boss every time.

## Decision
- **Copy-Paste (mini-boss):**
  - When it spawns it reads the first three shooting spells of the wand in your hand, and **Copy Cast** fires them back last-first after a glitch flash. A bolt becomes an aimed shot, a spray becomes a spread, and an explosion becomes a ring where it lands.
  - **Ctrl+Z** jumps back to where it was. It then lags for 1.2 s and takes ×1.5 damage (a gold ring and "LAG").
  - **Phase 2**, at 50%: it splits into a ghost copy (the real one flickers), and adds Ctrl+V (buglings) and **Select All** (a 1.2 s box outline, then the box fills with short-lived shots).
- **The Infinite Loop (boss):**
  - **Phase 1:** laps, Tail Volley and Lap Charge. The **head is armoured**: 100 armour, and blast breaks it. The body is soft and passes 40% of its damage straight through (forwarded hits skip armour, via the `World.SOFT` hit flag).
  - **Phase 2**, at 66%:
    - Segments can be broken. Each costs the Loop 3% of its HP, and every three break off a **Loop Jr.** that hunts on its own.
    - while(true) rings join the moves.
    - The arena's four rune pylons: four pulses **derail** it for 4 s at ×2 damage.
  - **Phase 3**, at 30%: the head alone chases you, leaving a trail of shots that burn for 1.2 s.
- **Both bosses:** a screen **shockwave** on phase changes. A screen-space ring shader stays hidden when idle and respects the Flash setting.
- **Balance bot fixes found by the bench stalls:**
  - The planner values a lone Rune Burst ×0.3 and a Static Cone ×0.7 when cast straight from the wand; both keep full value when delivered.
  - The bot presses in until its hits land again after 8 s without damage. Before, it walked in for only 2 s, so a short-range wand stalled a run for 900 s.

## Consequences
- **Tests:** 8 new boss tests (`tests/unit/test_bosses_d7.gd`).
- **Bench:**
  - Editing bot 80%, no stalls; mini-boss about 80 s, boss about 39 s.
  - Never-editing bot 10%: one win, one seed short of the 15–30% target.
- **Open:** the 1,300-bullet stress test sits at about 10 ms against its 10 ms budget. A/B timings (D4 10.4 ms, D5 10.0 ms, D7 about 10.4 ms) show noise on a container that is slower today, not a regression.
  - It picks its enemies as `DEFS.keys()[i % 4]`, so D4's new entries silently changed its roster. Pinning the original four kinds is proposed but not applied.
  - See `HANDOFF.md`.
