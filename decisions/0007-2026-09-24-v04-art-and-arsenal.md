# 0007. v0.4: one art direction drawn in code, a hipster hero, and a redesigned arsenal

- Date: 2026-09-24
- Status: Accepted

## Context
Bar's feedback after the first phone tests:
- "fix the graphics and art work, improve", with all four problem areas picked: characters too small and plain, the room not filling the screen, plain menus and title, and a consistent look everywhere
- "the spells and relics should be designed and arted better"
- "Reinvent the main character to be a hipster". After the first attempt he sent a reference picture (a stock pixel hipster) and asked for that direction.

He chose to keep the art drawn in code (original and deterministic, ADR 0002) over asset packs or an artist.

## Decision
- **One style, in `scripts/art/style.gd`.**
  - A single palette of hue-shifted 5-step ramps plus the UI colors.
  - Shading moves a pixel along its own ramp; it never mixes toward black or white.
  - Outlines are "sel-out": the neighbouring color's darkest step on the lit side, INK on the shadow side.
  - Actors are saturated and the floor is desaturated, so actors always read on top of it.
- **Art is ASCII rows on those ramps** (`PixelArt.paint` / `layered`), built from parts so frames animate by moving parts. A contact-sheet tool (`tools/artsheet.sh`) is the review loop.
- **The hero is a hipster mage.**
  - The look: a swept-up quiff, teal shades, a full dark beard, a yellow shirt with a red tie and teal suspenders, slim red trousers.
  - The pixels are our own, drawn in the spirit of Bar's reference rather than copied from it (the reference is a stock image).
  - The Copy-Paste boss is a glitched copy of him, and the app icon is his bust.
- **Enemies are redrawn at 14–23 px** (the Loop has an open-jaw telegraph frame). Hitboxes stay in `Enemy.DEFS`, so the art size does not change gameplay.
- **Rooms fill the phone.**
  - Rooms get 14 by 9 tiles of painted surroundings (canopy, trunks, rubble) and a vignette instead of black bars.
  - The room templates keep their size, so balance and the tile-based tests do not move.
  - The set pieces are sprites: archway doors, the reward altar, fountain, merchant, anvil and torches.
- **The arsenal is redesigned** (`research/arsenal-v04.md`):
  - Each shooting spell has one role and its own projectile sprite (one atlas, one MultiMesh draw call).
  - 4 new shooting spells, 2 new boosts and a new trigger.
  - Relics move from flat stat bumps to effects that change how you play: 8 new, 1 merged, 1 cut.
  - Synergy tags lean the reward rolls toward the build you already have.
  - Every item gets an illustrated 16×16 icon whose frame shape tells its kind.
- **The UI shares one look:** lit panels, bevelled buttons, a pulsing primary button, rarity-banded cards with tag chips, and a parallax title.

## Consequences
- Anyone can regenerate every sprite, tile, icon and the app icon from code. The art sheets make review quick.
- New art must use Style ramps. A new item needs an `IconSpells` / `IconRelics` entry, and `test_run` checks that it has an icon.
- The balance bench still holds after the arsenal changes: 70% survival, mini-boss about 58 s, boss about 41 s.
- A run saved with the cut relics (Cache Line, Keen Scope) loads without them.
