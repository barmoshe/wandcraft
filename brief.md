# wandcraft — brief

Immutable once written. Track changes as decisions, not edits.

- **One-liner:** Wandcraft, the pixel-art wand-programming roguelite prototyped as a claude.ai artifact (v1-v5), rebuilt from scratch as a real mobile game and published on the App Store and Google Play.
- **For / job-to-be-done:** Bar wants to publish his own game, a polished and well-built one. It started as a gift for Daniel and became a Magicraft-style game worth shipping.
- **Bar's words (2026-09-24):** "The next version will be a mobile game for iOS I want to publish. Recreate it from scratch, research best practices, and [tell me] if we should use Swift."
- **v1 "done":** The game is live on both stores: free download, World 1 playable, one non-consumable "Full Game" unlock for Worlds 2-5.
- **Constraints:**
  - iOS and Android at launch.
  - Original content only: mechanics may be inspired by Magicraft, names, text, numbers and art may not.
  - Bar has a Mac but no Apple Developer account yet.
  - The code lives in `bar_builds/projects/wandcraft/`.
- **Visual style:** Rich pixel art with dynamic lighting and bloom, as in v4/v5. HUD layout like Magicraft (stacked wand rows with round sockets, relic column, HP/MP bottom-left), in our own "arcane grimoire" skin (stone, gold filigree, gem sockets).
- **After-demo intent:** Ship and maintain as Bar's own title. It may also serve as a portfolio piece.
- **Open questions:**
  - The unlock price (around $4.99?).
  - The final title: "Wandcraft" still needs a store and trademark check.
  - Organization or individual Apple account.
