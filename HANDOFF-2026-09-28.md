# Wandcraft: handoff (2026-09-28)

**What happened:** Bar asked for research-first design work, done autonomously: a better story, more NPCs, more graphics and more worlds. The plan (`~/.claude/plans/research-design-folio-autonomous-cozy-avalanche.md`) went research, then design, then build. Five research agents wrote sourced notes (`research/world3/`), merged and scored in `research/world3-0.20.md`. Art, sound and voice agents then worked in parallel on files they owned, while the lead built the game logic. The decision record is ADR 0028.

## What's new in 0.20
- **World 3, the Kernel:**
  - Two room themes and eight new Style ramps.
  - The Page Leak, the Dangling Pointer and the Interrupt (it suspends a wand spell).
  - Data Race (two threads that must fall together).
  - The Glitch, with diff rows, a stack unwind and revert glyphs.
  - Kernel music, two ambience beds and 17 effects.
- **Residents** (`game/scripts/sim/residents.gd`): Grep, Hotfix and Cache, caged one per world (the "Someone Caged" door), then living in the Workshop.
  - Arcs gated on play, reactions to the last run, and a "!" when a beat is ready.
  - Services: Search, the Skin Forge (wand skins for Bits), and the Stacks (12 Lost Pages).
- **Story:**
  - Six new commit logs and a wall clock that runs down (16:59:57, :58, :59).
  - The ending moved to the Glitch.
  - A true ending, fix forward, after Grep's confession, then a blameless post-mortem in the Workshop.
  - `research/story.md` has the 0.20 section.
- **Graphics:**
  - Surroundings per theme (roots, chimneys, shelves, rings).
  - Resident portraits in the speech box.
  - Bosses compile in row by row.
  - Wand skins on the held wand.

## How to look at it
- **Screenshots** (`tools/shots.sh <name> <args>`):
  - Kernel rooms: `--demo --world=3 --step=3` and `--step=7`.
  - Bosses: `--demo --kind=boss --world=3 --loadout=strong` and `--kind=mini`.
  - The Workshop with residents: `--screen=hub --runs=6 --residents=grep,hotfix,cache --pages=5`.
  - Resident screens: `--screen=grep|hotfix|cache` with `--residents=...`.
  - The choice and the descent: `--screen=commit`, `--screen=world --to=2`.
- **Art:** `tools/artsheet.sh kernel` and `tools/artsheet.sh rooms`.
- **Balance:** `tools/balance.sh` now also runs `test_kernel_balance`, which starts in the Kernel with a mid-game kit.
