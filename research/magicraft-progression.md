# Magicraft's progression: research for Wandcraft

Researched 2026-09-27, at Bar's request, from the Steam store, the developer's posts, the official BWIKI, the fan wiki, Steam guides, discussions and reviews. Sources are listed at the end. Anything inferred is marked as an inference.

## At a glance
- **Game:** Magicraft, by Wave Game, published by bilibili.
- **Release:** Early Access on 2 Nov 2023, 1.0 on 2 Nov 2024 [1].
- **Reception:** 93% positive across 16,899 Steam reviews on 27 Sep 2026 [37].
- **Size at 1.0:** 5 chapters, 100 spells, 100 wands, 9 sets (starting loadouts), 80 relics, 60 curses, 35 potions and 234 monsters [1].
- **Run length:** about 30–60 minutes, and close to 2 hours on high difficulties [35][19][20].

## 1. How a run is built
- **Chapters:** a run has 5 chapters, and you choose rooms by picking a door, as in Hades. The doors show what reward is behind them; a curse can hide the symbols [15][36].
- **Room types** [15]:
  - **Spell room:** its rarity rises with chapter and difficulty.
  - **Relic room:** pick one of three.
  - **Max-HP room:** a fixed +29.
  - **Gold room:** 14–25 coins.
  - **Shop:** a wand at the current chapter's tier, 4 spells, keys and HP, with rerolls.
  - **Processing room:** merge 3 copies of a spell into one with a higher star, or reroll spells.
  - **Fountain:** heals 80% of your HP.
  - **Key rooms:** mini-games and cursed chests.
  - **Elite and boss rooms:** one of each per chapter.
  - **No-hit room:** unlocks only if you beat the boss without taking damage. It sells epic relics that you pay for with max HP.
- **Shortcut room:** optional since 1.0. It replaces 4 rooms and gives one of each reward [1][25].
- **Bosses:** each chapter draws from a pool; in Early Access Chapter 1 had 6 candidates [16]. Chapter 5 is a boss rush [1].
- **Scaling is steep:** Chapter 1 bosses have 650–1,700 HP, Chapter 2 bosses 3,000–6,000, and Chapter 4's has 290,000 [16].

## 2. Progression within a run
- **Starting state:** you begin with 1 wand slot, 10 backpack slots and 60 HP [15].
- **Spells:** three copies of a spell merge into one with a higher star [17].
- **Epic spells:** they appear about 1% of the time. Nightmare 1–2 multiplies that chance by 1.5, and Nightmare 3 by 2 [16].
- **Relic sets:** since 1.1, relics come in series with a set bonus [2].
- **Curses:** most come from cursed chests you choose to open [15].

## 3. Progression between runs: the camp
- **Currencies:** three of them (Crystals, Ancient Blood and Chaotic Core). Since 1.0 they can be exchanged for one another [1][16].
- **Vivian:** talents. These include extra wand slots, up to +60 max HP, starting coins, backpack space, and healing when you enter a room [16].
- **Lyon:** research. Springs, potion capacity, shop rerolls up to ×8, extra keys, and damage stats since 1.1 [1][5][16].
- **Lilian:** makes spell pickups a choice of 3, and sells themed packs that add spells and relics to the pool [16].
- **Gina:** spell bans, up to 44 [1][11].
- **Leah:** sets, which work like classes. They unlock through feats such as "stack poison to 1,500", "clear Chapter 3 on Hard" or "kill 300 enemies with one weapon" [1][16].

## 4. Difficulty
- **Tiers:** Easy, Normal, Hard, then Nightmare 1–3 [17].
- **Hard:** opens after you clear Chapter 3 on Normal. It raises enemy HP and changes some attack patterns [16].
- **Content behind difficulty:** chapters and areas are gated behind it. In Early Access, Chapter 4 existed only on Hard [8][16][22].
- **Rewards:** Hard gives about +50% resources and Nightmare +100%. Nightmare also brings mutant enemies [21][9].
- **Endless mode:** promised since 2023 and still not out [8][12].

## 5. What players say
**They like:**
- An unlock every 1–2 runs [30].
- Meta upgrades that are "cheap enough… but slow enough" [34].
- Bans that stop the spell pool diluting their builds [33].

**They dislike:**
- **Slow spell unlocks** tied to late bosses, bought with the same currency as stat upgrades [27].
- **Grind** [31].
- **Long runs:** multiple waves per room, slow shops and slow puzzles [28].
- **High difficulties that only add HP** ("bullet sponges") [20][23][29].
- **A final DPS check** that wipes out a build you spent 30–45 minutes on [19].
- **Repetition** after about 20 runs [30].

## 6. How it changed over time
- **0.8:** added Nightmare and bans [8].
- **June 2024 dev log:** the developer named run length, resource imbalance and pool bloat as the problems. Resource exchange, shortcut rooms and spell locks were the fixes [3].
- **1.0:** Chapter 5 became a boss rush because "magicians can generally complete your build in the first four chapters" [1].
- **1.1:** added relic set bonuses and a recycler, and announced that a mobile port was under way [2].
- **1.2.42 (Sep 2026):** new bosses, and bans that cost crystals only [11].

## Lessons for Wandcraft (priority order)
1. **Keep rooms short.** Budget about 50–60 seconds per room, with at most two short waves and one-step shops and forges. Length is Magicraft's top complaint [28][20]. Wandcraft's 20 rooms and 4 bosses fit about 25 minutes.
2. **Make heat change the rules, not HP.** Borrow curse-style modifiers [15] and pay more rewards per tier [21]. Nightmare's "bullet sponge" is the most criticised thing in the game [20][29].
3. **Open content early, and keep it apart from power.** Magicraft's biggest meta complaint is slow spell unlocks paid for with the same currency as stats [27]. Wandcraft's goal unlocks already avoid that; keep them landing within runs 1–5.
4. **Let the pool be steered.** A short ban list plus a reroll is enough on mobile [3][1][33]. Wandcraft already has BAN and a reroll in the shop.
5. **The final boss should test the build, not wipe it out.** Show what Deadlock asks for early, and make sure crowd builds and single-target builds can both beat it [19].
6. **Add a no-hit bonus after bosses.** It is cheap to build and gives skilled players a reward that isn't just a harder setting [15][26]. Wandcraft's Untouched door is close; tie one to boss fights.
7. **Guarantee rewards at milestones.** The mini-boss gives a rare spell and the boss gives a choice of three relics [26]. Wandcraft's boss orbs do this already; make the rarity explicit.
8. **Keep permanent stats small and capped, and give the second wand slot as a goal.** Magicraft's single starting slot, bought up at the camp, makes early runs weak [15][16].
